Download every OpenStreetMap (OSM) feature in an area that meets tag filters into a GeoPackage, such as roads, buildings, water, land use or points of interest. Returns the file written, its layers and columns, and the keys dropped. To download the shape of an object found with osmsearch, use osmgetobjects instead.

- One kind of thing a call: download roads, water and buildings in separate calls, each to its own file.
- One kind of thing is often tagged in several ways: give them all. When unsure which, look with osmprobe and a "term".
- For points of interest, use geometry "point" rather than keeping nodes alone: many places are mapped as areas, and would be lost.
- A large download is split into parts by the tool itself; one too large for OSM's public servers is refused before anything is downloaded, with how many features it would hold. The answer says what could not be downloaded, if anything.
- Features crossing the edge of the area are kept whole, not cut.

- The file has a layer for each kind of geometry it holds: points, lines and polygons. Coordinates are in EPSG:4326, as OSM's are, and no geometry is simplified.
- Every feature has osm_id, name and category; the keys the filters name and those worth recording for that kind of thing are kept as columns too, with values as OSM has them. To keep a dropped key, download again with "fields".
