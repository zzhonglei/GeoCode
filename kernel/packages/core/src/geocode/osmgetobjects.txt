Download OpenStreetMap (OSM) objects by id into a GeoPackage, such as the shape of a campus, a lake, a park or a district found with osmsearch. Returns the file written, its layers and columns, and the keys dropped. To download every feature of a kind in an area, use osmgetfeatures instead.

- The file has a layer for each kind of geometry it holds: points, lines and polygons. Coordinates are in EPSG:4326, as OSM's are, and no geometry is simplified.
- Every feature has osm_id, name and category; the tags that say what it is and those worth recording for its kind are kept as columns too, with values as OSM has them. To keep a dropped key, download again with "fields".
